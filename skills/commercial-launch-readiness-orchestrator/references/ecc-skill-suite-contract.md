# ECC Skill Suite Contract

Use this contract when this skill is edited or combined with `skill-optimizer` and `coding-judgment`.

- This skill owns one-repo commercial launch readiness audits, market/customer research, launch decisions, outreach plans, and safe P0/P1 commercial fixes.
- `coding-judgment` owns repo-side fix discipline inside the safe fix lane.
- `skill-optimizer` owns changes to this skill, its prompts, report template, evidence schema, and ECC routing.
- Launch claims must stay in the correct proof bucket: hosted/live, local, repo artifact, candidate/shadow, or roadmap.
- Validate completed evidence with `scripts/validate_launch_evidence.py` before portfolio comparison or commercial-ready claims.
- Stop before production deploys, credential changes, payment changes, destructive actions, live outreach, or costly worker execution without approval.
