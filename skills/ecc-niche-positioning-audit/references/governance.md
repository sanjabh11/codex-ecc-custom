# Governance and Operating Cadence

## Roles and independence

Record auditor, product decision owner, evidence owner, experiment owner, and
approver. The person who owns a load-bearing claim should not be its sole
adversarial reviewer. Record conflicts of interest, incentives, sponsor pressure,
and unresolved dissent. ISO 19011 is used as an audit-governance analogy, not as
certification of market research.

## Human gates

User approval is required before Phase 5. Human approval is also required before
using a supported hypothesis in external positioning, changing a price/distribution
claim, or declaring a final GO. Automation may flag, score, and block; it may not
silently promote evidence or override a gate.

## Adversarial review

Persist three independent records per required artifact: skeptic, missing
perspective, and contrarian. Each has reviewer ID, context, challenge,
resolution status, notes, and timestamp. Fatal flaws or unresolved contradictions
force RECYCLE or STOP.

## Cadence

| Cadence | Review |
|---|---|
| weekly/biweekly | active experiment results and new first-party signals |
| monthly | competitor movement, evidence freshness, and drift flags |
| quarterly | full evidence refresh, hypothesis re-validation, segment re-ranking |

Stale evidence is never load-bearing until re-checked. Keep a decision log for
scope changes, skipped phases, approvals, and remediation closure.
